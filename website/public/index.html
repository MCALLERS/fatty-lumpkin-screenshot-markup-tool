A FEW SMALL DETAILS

Good to know.

What do I need?+

An Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later, an iPad, and a trusted local network shared by both devices. Apple Pencil makes drawing natural; finger drawing is also available. The native iPad app requires iPadOS 18 or later. Install the native app through TestFlight, or use the included Safari companion.

Does it send my screenshots to an AI?+

No. Fatty Lumpkin saves a marked-up PNG and copies it to your Mac’s clipboard. You decide whether to paste or upload it into another app, and when to send it. That app’s policies then apply.

Where are my screenshots saved?+

On your Mac, in Documents → Screenshot Tool. Originals live in Inbox; completed PNGs live in Ready. If you have enabled iCloud or another Documents sync service, it may independently sync those files. Read the privacy and storage details.

Is it really free?+

Yes. The beta is free to download and the source code is available under the MIT license. It’s a small independent project, still growing. If something is rough around the edges, tell us on GitHub without sharing private screenshots or pairing keys.
